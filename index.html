Update 5/4/26

Hi everyone, I'm back! Decided to take another break from updating the site because I was so busy with school. It's an exciting time, and during this period of unemployment (which hopefully isn't too long), I'll be trying to make more puzzles for the website.

If you're interested in playing a fast-paced roguelike first-person shooter where you use wacky weapons to defeat enemies, then I'm sure you'll love our game!

I'm sad that my time in college has ended, but I'm excited for the stuff I make in the future. Hopefully more crossword puzzles. Happy solving!
